Our Story

Where Science Meets
Artistry

Founded in 2012, Adam & Eve Medical Aesthetics has grown to become one of Scottsdale's most trusted and celebrated medical spas. Our team of licensed nurses, nurse practitioners, and master estheticians combine advanced medical training with an artist's eye for natural, beautiful results.

"Our goal is to help you achieve timeless beauty through natural enhancements — using advanced technology and industry expertise to bring out your best self."

We've earned Allergan's prestigious Black Diamond status — placing us in the top 1% of aesthetic practices nationwide for Botox, Juvederm, and Voluma. But more than any award, we're proud of the trust our patients place in us every day.

Our commitment to natural results means your friends will notice you look amazing — not that you've had work done.
